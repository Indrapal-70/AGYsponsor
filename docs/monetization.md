# Monetization Model

AgentSponsor connects developers with direct sponsorship revenue.

## Revenue Model
- Sponsors pay for impressions (CPM) or specific interactions within the developer tool.
- Revenue is generated from active campaigns targeting developer demographics.

## Developer Share
- Developers receive an **80% share** of gross revenue generated by their installations.
- Earnings are calculated per impression and accrued in the developer dashboard.

## Advertiser Guidelines
- Ads must be relevant to developers (e.g., dev tools, cloud services).
- Ads must be non-intrusive (no pop-ups, no tracking pixels, plain text or simple markdown only).
